# Doctor appointment preparation article

**Practice project / Healthcare writing sample.** Created for this portfolio.

A patient education sample about preparing for an appointment, asking questions, and keeping track of follow-up instructions. Written and edited using [Humanizer](https://github.com/blader/humanizer).

## Files

- `article.md`: Finished article with source links and a printable notes sheet.
- `index.html`: Responsive HTML article with the portfolio theme, contents navigation, a print button, and SEO metadata.
- `seo.json`: SEO title, description, keyword candidates, audience, and structured data.
- `sources.md`: References and the statements they support.

## Read and use

[Read the article](https://razeljade28.github.io/Portfolio/samples/doctor-appointment-preparation/index.html) or find it in [Sample projects](https://razeljade28.github.io/Portfolio/#/sample-projects).

Open `index.html` locally to preview it without a build or dependency installation. The print button prints the article and blank notes tables. The theme button shares the portfolio's light/dark preference when viewed on the same site.

To adapt the article for a healthcare business, confirm its appointment procedures and add appropriate booking and contact links. Update the canonical URL and publisher details for the new destination. Credit a medical reviewer only after review has taken place.

Keyword phrases are editorial candidates. Search volume, ranking difficulty, and performance have not been measured. Humanizer was used to edit the prose; source verification was handled separately.

## How this sample was made

Created to practise patient education writing. NHS, NIH, MedlinePlus, and AHRQ guidance informed the outline. The draft was edited with Humanizer and turned into an article with an appointment notes sheet, SEO title, and description.
